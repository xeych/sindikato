About Sweet Crumbs

Sweet Crumbs Cake Shop started as a small home bakery with one simple goal: to bring delicious, freshly baked cakes to every celebration. We keep our menu focused on five signature flavors — Ube, Pineapple, Apple, Strawberry, and Chocolate — so that every cake we bake gets our full attention and care.

From birthdays to anniversaries, or just because it's a Tuesday, we believe every moment deserves something sweet.
